The author suggests 3 passes in reading a paper, each successive pass taking more care and more time, and each next pass only being attempted if necessary.
# First pass
Answer these questions, after reading the title, abstract, introduction, and conclusion carefully, as well as sections and sub-section headings.
- What type of paper is it?
	- 
- Which papers is it related to and what does it use?
- Are the assumptions valid?
- What are the main contributions of the paper?
- Is it well written?
# Second Pass
Read more carefully, but ignore proofs, and just look at key points.
